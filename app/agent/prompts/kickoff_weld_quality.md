INCIDENT — WELD QUALITY REVIEW (different machine)
This incident comes from a laboratory pedestal spot welder (RSW-LAB), not from RSW-01.
The RSW-01 process knowledge and detectors described above do not apply to it, and the
tools that read RSW-01 records (query_timeseries, recall_cases) are not available here.

The in-line monitor flagged ONE weld for review. The incident summary below holds
everything that was logged for it: the setpoints, the welding current and electrode force
sampled during the weld, and how welds made at the same setpoints are distributed.

Decide what happened to this weld: was it an acceptable weld, or did a defect occur —
and if so, which one, by what physical mechanism? A weld that turns out to be fine is a
valid answer.

A technician can photograph the welded coupon on request — either face of it.
