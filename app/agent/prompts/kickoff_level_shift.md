INCIDENT — LEVEL SHIFT (this is NOT an alarm burst)
A day-level control chart tripped: the workday median of a process variable moved outside
limits estimated from stable production days. There is no alarm flood here — the signal is
quiet and slow. Point-wise anomaly detection does not catch this class of problem.

Start by asking: how far did it move and in which direction, did other variables move with
it, and is the within-day behaviour consistent with a changed operating condition (recipe,
setpoint, tooling) rather than a sudden fault?
